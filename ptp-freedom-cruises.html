Frequently Asked Questions

What happens after I submit the registration form?

I'll personally review your request and contact you within 24–48 hours with the next steps, including cruise availability (if needed), registration payment instructions and any additional details.

Does my cruise cost extra to use this service?

Your cruise fare is the same whether you book through the P2P group or directly with the cruise line. To participate in the exclusive P2P Freedom Cruise experiences, you'll pay a non-refundable registration fee. You can book through the P2P group for the lower registration fee, or book independently and participate by paying the independent registration fee.

What is included with my registration?

Registration includes access to all scheduled P2P Freedom Cruise activities, including Meet & Greet Sailaway, Travel Symposium sessions, Behind the Curtain, Design Your Paradise, optional Slot Pull & Casino Q&A and the private Facebook community.

Does each person in my cabin have to pay a registration fee?

No. If you book through the P2P group, the registration fee is $99 per cabin and covers all guests in the cabin.

Do I pay a registration fee for each cruise?

Yes. Each P2P Freedom Cruise has its own registration fee.

Can friends or family travel with me if they don't participate?

Absolutely. Anyone is welcome to sail with you. Only registered participants will have access to the exclusive P2P Freedom Cruise activities.

When will I receive the final schedule?

A detailed itinerary, meeting locations, and activity times will be emailed to all registered participants before the cruise.

Are registration fees refundable?

No. Registration fees are non-refundable because planning and event preparation begin shortly after registration.

Are cruise deposits refundable?

Cruise deposit policies are determined by the cruise line and the fare you choose. Some deposits are refundable, while others are non-refundable. I'll let you know your options before you book.

How do I pay for my cruise?

Once you've selected your cruise, you'll receive a secure payment link to complete your reservation. If you're registering for P2P Freedom Cruise activities only, I'll provide separate registration payment instructions after reviewing your registration.

Do I have to book my cruise through the P2P group?

No. You're welcome to book through the P2P group for the lowest registration fee or book independently and still participate in the P2P Freedom Cruise experiences by paying the independent registration fee of $199 per person.
